Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Information you provide

Golf IQ collects account details such as your sign-in name and email, and the golf information you choose to enter: goals, profile answers, round scores and reflections, practice results, natural ball flight, club carry distances, spin preferences, saved shot choices and modeled results, preset challenge reactions, and friend connections or invitations you choose to create or accept. We use this information to operate your account, personalize coaching and practice, and save your progress across devices.

Your public player profile and daily competition

Daily play can be explored without an account. Signing in lets you put an official result on the leaderboard. The leaderboard shows your chosen player name, optional profile photo and country, and competition information such as modeled score, distance to the pin and rank. Your optional account bio is private by default. In versions that support public player bios, you can choose “Make this bio public” to make it publicly visible, including to visitors who are not signed in. You can turn sharing off or edit or remove the bio in Player profile. Existing private bios are not published automatically. Your email, private coaching answers and full personal bag are not displayed on leaderboards. Individual shot or club details may be included when you choose to share a challenge or result. You choose whether to add a photo or country and can edit or remove them in Player profile.

Finalized World and private challenge wins contribute to aggregate trophy counts on your public player profile. These counts do not expose private opponents, shot choices or detailed contest results. A private win already earned by another golfer may remain in that golfer’s aggregate count after a contest or its creator’s account is deleted.

Friends, invitations and reminders

You can send a friend request from a player profile or share a friend-invitation link. New friend links can connect several recipients to the sender until their expiry or service limit; each person chooses whether to accept. Older friend invitations remain single-use. Private challenge invitations do not connect people as friends automatically.

Accepted friends can see whether you have recorded an official shot for today, along with your competition result. You can share a private closest-to-the-pin challenge link for a selected hole. New group challenges allow up to 16 signed-in players; older two-player invitations still accept one friend. A complete group link can be used by several eligible recipients until its displayed capacity or deadline is reached, so share it only with your intended group. The preview shows the selected hole, conditions, creator’s public player name, capacity, number of joined players and deadline. It does not expose the participant list, results, reactions or full shot choices. Opening a link does not join, submit a shot or connect friends automatically. Participants see shared conditions, public player profiles, played status, distances to the pin, private standings and preset reactions shared within the contest. Each player’s full shot choice and coaching read are returned only to that player. New group play closes at the deadline chosen by the creator, up to seven days; older two-player invitations close after seven days. Expiry does not itself delete the account-linked contest. A rematch creates a new invitation and does not join previous participants automatically. These shots do not change the official daily World leaderboard or streak.

Friends Cups are named private groups of up to 16 golfers that can renew every seven days. They combine eligible saved official Daily Hole finishes into best-five standings without creating another official attempt. Members can see public player profiles, played-today status, distances, points, standings and preset reactions. Anyone with a complete valid Cup invitation can preview the Cup name, host’s public profile, member count, capacity, scoring and eligibility dates, recurrence, joining status and expiry. The preview does not expose the member roster, results, reactions or full shot choices. Opening the invitation does not join a Cup, connect friends or submit a shot; each golfer chooses whether to join.

Your in-app Activity stores friend requests, connection confirmations, private challenge invitations and final-result notices, with timestamps and read status. Invitations and results remain available there when push alerts are off.

Friend invitations and result alerts are optional remote notifications delivered through Apple Push Notification service (APNs). When you enable them, we associate your device’s push token and an app installation identifier with your signed-in Golf IQ account, notification preference and delivery environment. We also keep basic delivery-status records to send alerts and troubleshoot delivery. Alerts can include event text such as another golfer’s public player name and may appear on your Lock Screen, depending on your iPhone settings. Push tokens are not displayed to other players. You can turn these alerts off in Golf IQ or iPhone Settings.

Daily tee-time reminders remain optional local notifications at the time you choose. Golf IQ asks for iPhone notification permission when you enable alerts or set a reminder. You can change or remove a reminder in the app, or turn off notifications in iPhone Settings. Reminder text does not include your profile, golf history, location or purchase details.

Photos, videos and AI features

When you choose content for an AI feature, selected swing-video frames, scorecard images, round details and related golf-profile context may be sent through Golf IQ services to OpenAI to produce an analysis or read. Before publishing or updating the public parts of a player profile, the submitted display name, profile photo and any bio you choose to publish may also be sent to OpenAI for automated safety moderation. A bio kept private is not sent for this public-profile safety check. Moderation results help us identify content that should not be published. OpenAI processes this information under its applicable service terms and privacy practices.

Service providers and subscriptions

We use Supabase for account authentication, database and file storage; OpenAI for AI features and safety moderation; RevenueCat and Apple for purchases and subscription entitlements; and AppsFlyer for app analytics and install-attribution measurement. These providers process information needed to deliver their services, subject to their applicable terms and privacy and security obligations. Apple handles App Store payment details; Golf IQ does not receive your full payment-card information.

Usage, attribution and tracking choices

We collect app-interaction events and technical information to understand feature use, measure installs, support subscriptions and improve reliability. This may include device or app identifiers, purchase information and diagnostics. Golf IQ does not sell your personal information. Where advertising tracking permission is needed, we use Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can allow or deny that permission in iOS Settings; core golf features do not require you to allow tracking. Some analytics and attribution measurements can operate without advertising tracking permission.

Reports, blocking and safety

You can report objectionable public profile content and block a player from public player profiles and the leaderboard experience. Reports may include the reported entry, the reason, relevant public profile content and identifiers needed to investigate abuse. Blocking hides the player’s profile and prevents friend and private challenge interactions; it does not change other golfers' scores or the underlying competition results. We use safety information to review reports, enforce our rules and protect the service. For safety concerns or help, contact support@golfiq.app.

Storage, retention and deletion

We retain account and golf information for as long as needed to provide the service and saved history, unless you delete it or request account deletion. You can sign out, manage subscriptions, restore purchases or delete your account from Profile. Account deletion removes your account-linked cloud profile, stored profile-photo revisions and saved app information tied to the account, including its activity, awards and push-device registrations. Deleting the creator’s account removes a private closest-to-the-pin contest and its detailed participation records; aggregate private-win counts already earned by other golfers may remain on their profiles. Daily competition results may remain in an anonymized form so historical standings stay accurate. We may retain limited information when needed for security, abuse investigations, disputes or legal obligations. Apple and payment providers may retain transaction records under their own obligations.

Deleting an account removes its public player profile and account-linked Cup invitations, reactions, awards, activity and alert-device registrations. It removes the account link from Cup membership and ends participation in future periods. The Cup name and group record, recorded scores, frozen standings and other golfers’ earned Cup awards remain for the remaining members, without the deleted golfer’s public profile. This also applies when the deleted account created the Cup; its invitation links then become unusable.

Your choices and public content

You can update your player profile and remove your photo from within the app. Signing out does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage cancellation in your Apple ID subscription settings. A privacy or deletion request can also be sent to support@golfiq.app. Other people may already have viewed, downloaded or shared content you made public, and removing it from Golf IQ cannot remove copies they independently retain.

Policy updates and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes and will show the new update date. For privacy questions, data requests or deletion help, contact support@golfiq.app.
