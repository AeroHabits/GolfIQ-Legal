Privacy Policy

Last updated May 21, 2026

Golf IQ collects the information needed to run your private golf account: sign-in details, onboarding answers, scorecards, round totals, swing clips, AI reads, practice progress, subscription status, and basic device diagnostics.

We use this information to personalize your Golf IQ plan, save history across devices, provide AI swing and round reads, process subscriptions through Apple and RevenueCat, and improve reliability.

Golf IQ does not sell your personal information. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites for advertising. If that ever changes, we will ask for permission with Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before tracking.

AI features may send uploaded scorecard images, swing frames, and related profile context to service providers so the app can produce your read. We keep prompts focused on golf analysis and avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

You can restore purchases, manage subscriptions, sign out, or delete your account from Profile. Deleting your account removes your Golf IQ cloud profile and saved app data tied to that account. Some payment records remain with Apple as required for billing, tax, fraud prevention, and legal compliance.

For privacy questions or deletion help, contact support@golfiq.app.
